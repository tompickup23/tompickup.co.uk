# Public debates release

Tom authorised publication of the Preston, Burnley and Blackburn articles after a
political and factual sense-check, followed by adaptations for Reform UK Burnley.

The copy records participation, the organiser's event titles, topics and audience
questions. Broad consensus wording and individual speech paraphrases were removed
to avoid implying policy agreement or attributing a position beyond the evidence.
Original press links and Light Foundation reports remain available to readers.

Each article's job is a documentary event record. No headline statistic accompanies
a named person. No synthetic imagery or unsupported quotation is used. Presentation
lint has no BLOCK findings. Its reading-grade warnings count raw gallery HTML;
“the left” describes a photograph position. Negative headline suggestions are
overruled because these are factual event titles, not click campaigns.

Release is based on source origin/main f597cc8, with current feeds and published
portfolio work preserved. Only debate content, 17 used images, gallery support,
category navigation and supporting research are included. No local LGR drafts or
other unrelated changes are bundled. The live source repository uses the Pages
workflow; no legacy-mirror deployment is needed.

Validation: Astro build and parked-section guards; built house-style sweep; image
references; archive dates and links; desktop/mobile browser checks. Public URL
verification follows the Pages workflow.
