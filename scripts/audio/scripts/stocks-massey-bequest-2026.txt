Stocks Massey twenty twenty-six: thirty-four thousand pounds for Burnley groups and students.

The Edward Stocks Massey Bequest Fund approved thirty-four thousand pounds for twenty twenty-six to twenty-seven. That is twenty-eight thousand pounds in grants to eighteen local groups and services, and six thousand pounds for student scholarships.

Where the money goes.

County council projects receive five thousand pounds. Borough council projects receive five thousand pounds. The Mechanics Theatre receives five thousand pounds. Individuals and voluntary groups receive thirteen thousand pounds. And student scholarships receive six thousand pounds.

From the county council's share, Burnley Music Centre receives two thousand five hundred pounds, and the Lancashire County Library Service, Burnley District, receives two thousand five hundred pounds.

From the borough council's share, Townley Hall Museum and Art Gallery receives five thousand pounds.

The Mechanics Theatre receives five thousand pounds for Mechanics Programme Development.

Among voluntary groups: Building Bridges Burnley, five hundred pounds. Three five two Burnley Air Cadets, one thousand five hundred pounds. Burnley Film Makers, two hundred and fifty pounds. Burnley Light Opera Society, one thousand five hundred pounds. Burnley Municipal Choir, one thousand five hundred pounds. Burnley Symphony Orchestra, one thousand pounds. Garrick Theatre, one thousand pounds. Mid Pennine Arts, one thousand pounds. Sion Baptist Church, one thousand pounds. Saint Peter's Church of England Primary School, one thousand pounds. Saint Peter's Church, Saturday Morning Concert Committee, five hundred pounds. Saint Peter's Young Singers, five hundred pounds. Burnley Youth Theatre, one thousand two hundred and fifty pounds. And Gawthorpe Hall, five hundred pounds.

The Director of Finance approved the schedule on the first of July, twenty twenty-six. These are approved allocations, not payments. Five further applicants were allocated nothing. The scholarship amount is the category set aside in the report; the published papers do not split new awards from continuing ones.

The fund supports education, science, learning, music and the arts for the people of Burnley. Edward Stocks Massey died in nineteen oh nine, and his will was proved in nineteen ten. The grants for every year since twenty twenty-three, and the fund's history, are on the Stocks Massey page of the website.

The twenty twenty-six scholars.

Charlie Diamond and Jonah Morgan received the twenty twenty-six Stocks Massey scholarships. Burnley College announced them at its Awards for Excellence ceremony.

Congratulations to both of them. It is good to see this longstanding Burnley charity continuing to support young people alongside the organisations that contribute to the town's cultural and community life.
