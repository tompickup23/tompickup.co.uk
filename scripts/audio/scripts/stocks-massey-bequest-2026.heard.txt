Stocks Massey 2026, £34,000 for Burnley groups and students.
the Edward Stocks Massey Bequest Fund approved £34,000 for 2026-27. That is £28,000 in grants to 18 local groups and services, and £6,000 for student scholarships.
where the money goes.
County Council projects receive £5,000. Borough Council projects receive £5,000. The Mechanics Theatre receives £5,000. Individuals and voluntary groups receive £13,000. And student scholarships receive £6,000.
From the County Council's share, Burnley Music Centre receives £2,500, and the Lancashire County Library Service, Burnley District, receives £2,500.
From the Borough Council share, Townley Hall Museum and Art Gallery receives £5,000.
The Mechanics Theatre receives £5,000 for mechanics programme development.
Among voluntary groups, Building Bridges Burnley £500, 352 Burnley Air Cadets £1,500, Burnley Filmmakers £250, Burnley Light Opera Society £1,500, Burnley Municipal Choir £1,500, Burnley Symphony Orchestra £1,000, Garrick Theatre £1,000, Mid-Pennine Arts £1,000, Sion Baptist Church £1,000, St Peter's Church of England Primary School £1,000, St Peter's Church Saturday Morning Concert Committee £500, St Peter's Young Singers £500, Burnley Youth Theatre £1,250 and Gawthorpe Hall £500.
The Director of Finance approved the schedule on 1 July, 2026. These are approved allocations, not payments. Five further applicants were allocated nothing. The scholarship amount is the category set aside in the report. The published papers do not split new awards from continuing ones.
The fund supports education, science, learning, music and the arts for the people of Burnley. Edward Stocks Massey died in 1909 and his will was proved in 1910. The grants for every year since 2023 and the fund's history are on the Stocks Massey page of the website.
The 2026 Scholars
Charlie Diamond and Jonah Morgan received the 2026 Stocks Massey scholarships. Burnley College announced them at its awards for excellence ceremony.
Congratulations to both of them. It is good to see this long-standing Burnley charity continuing to support young people alongside the organisations that contribute to the town's cultural and community life.
