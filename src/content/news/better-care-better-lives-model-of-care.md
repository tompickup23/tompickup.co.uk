*By Tom Pickup, Lancashire county councillor for Padiham and Burnley West and Cabinet Member for Adult Social Care. The report is in my portfolio.*

On Thursday 1 October, Lancashire County Council's Cabinet agreed Better Care, Better Lives as the strategic model for adult social care in the county.

The resolution has two parts: that Lancashire's Model of Care "be agreed as the strategic model for Adult Social Care", and that "detailed design and implementation planning be progressed through co-production with residents, carers, communities, providers, and partners."<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## What the model is for

The model builds on the direction Cabinet endorsed in May 2026. It was shaped with people who use care and support, carers, families, staff, providers and community organisations.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

What they told us was consistent: trusted relationships, familiar faces, choice, and staying connected to their own area make the real difference. So the model starts with the person, not the service. In practice that means:

- help earlier, before things reach crisis point, with more emphasis on neighbourhood support
- local help that is easier to find, and joined-up conversations so nobody has to repeat their story to every new team
- more choice for people who need formal care, from flexible support at home to specialist housing and high-quality 24-hour care
- specialist services, including dementia care and help to regain independence after illness or a hospital stay, kept as close to home as possible

As I said when the report was published: "Good care is about much more than services. It is about helping people hold on to their independence, relationships, routines and sense of belonging."<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

## What it does not do

There has been understandable worry about closures, so I want to be exact.

The report to Cabinet does not propose or seek approval for the closure of any existing service or building. Any future proposal would need its own business case, the proper assessments, consultation and a separate decision.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

"Nothing in this report closes any service or building. The people who shaped this model will keep shaping it, alongside the staff and partners whose care people already value."

## What happens next

This is the start of the detailed design, not the end of the conversation. The design work will be done with the people who use care, the people who provide it, and the communities around them, which is exactly what Cabinet asked for.
