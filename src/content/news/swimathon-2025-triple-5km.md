*Retrospective note, published 28 September 2026. It records a charity swim from spring 2025, before I was elected.*

In spring 2025 I took on a Swimathon triple 5km: three separate 5km swims. I swam to raise awareness and funds for **Cancer Research UK** and **Marie Curie**.

The first 5km was on the afternoon of **Saturday 29 March 2025**, with Burnley Leisure & Culture. They posted the photograph above that day: "Well done to our Swimathon swimmers this afternoon." Swimathon UK shared it with my note that I would be doing the same again the next day.

The second 5km followed on **Sunday 30 March**. The third completed the set, and on **1 April 2025** Swimathon UK shared my "Triple 5km done" post.
