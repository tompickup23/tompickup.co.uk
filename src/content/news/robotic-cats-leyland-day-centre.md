Leyland day centre has three regulars who purr, meow and move their limbs. Misty, Phyllis and Ginge run on batteries. They are robotic cats, and the staff who hand them out say they are doing something that is hard to do with a leaflet or a tablet: calming people down.

I am the councillor who talks about technology more than most, so it is worth saying carefully what this is and is not.

## What the staff see

The cats are given to people who use the day centre, some of whom have dementia, to help them relax and to start a conversation. Helen Delbridge, a care assistant there, put it simply: "The more you stroke them and the more you interact with them then the more you get back off them."

Tanya Ambrose, another care assistant, described what happens when someone is getting anxious. Staff ask whether they would like a cat, and within moments the person is stroking it and visibly settling. One gentleman, she said, is so serene with a cat on his knee that he will sit quietly for ten minutes.

Olive Wells, who uses the centre, says she is more of a dog person. She also says this: "I come in, sit down and I get a cat and I feel more secure because I've got somebody to talk to."

What I said about it:

> Robotic pets are having a significant impact on people who attend the County Council's day centres and live in our care homes.
>
> They are helping to improve wellbeing, create meaningful connections for people and bring moments of joy.

## What this is not

It is not a replacement for Helen or Tanya. The cat works because a member of staff noticed someone was anxious, walked over, asked, and stayed. Technology in social care earns its place when it gives staff more of those moments, not when it is bought to need fewer of them. Whatever the cat costs, it is not the part that matters. A care assistant who knows which person wants one is, and that is the part worth paying for.

That is the test I will apply to every piece of technology that comes across my desk in this portfolio, including the things I am enthusiastic about.
