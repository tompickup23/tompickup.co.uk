Today is Lancashire Day. It marks the day in 1295 when Lancashire first sent representatives to Parliament.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

To mark it, Reform UK councillors at Lancashire County Council have made a short film about the county. You will hear County Councillor Stephen Atkinson, Leader of the council, with Ged Mirfin, Brian Moore and me, over pictures of Lancaster Castle, Hoghton Tower, Gawthorpe Hall in Burnley and Rivington Pike.<sup class="cite"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

The film is about what the county has been and what it can be again: the centre of the world in the Industrial Revolution, a proud farming heritage, and a county of people who build things and create things. My line in it is a simple one: Lancashire was one of the economic powerhouses of the world. The film ends where we want the county to go next, back to being an economic powerhouse.

Happy Lancashire Day.
