The Edward Stocks Massey Bequest Fund approved **£34,000 for 2026/27**: **£28,000 in grants to 18 local groups and services**, and **£6,000 for student scholarships**.

The Director of Finance approved the schedule on 1 July 2026. These are approved allocations, not payments. Five further applicants were allocated nothing. The scholarship amount is the category set aside in the report; the published papers do not split new awards from continuing ones.

The fund supports education, science, learning, music and the arts for the people of Burnley. Edward Stocks Massey died in 1909 and his will was proved in 1910. [See the grants for every year since 2023, and the fund's history](/stocks-massey/).

## The 2026 scholars

**Charlie Diamond and Jonah Morgan** received the 2026 Stocks Massey scholarships. [Burnley College announced them](https://www.burnley.ac.uk/video-gallery-celebrating-student-success-at-inspiring-awards-for-excellence-ceremony/) at its Awards for Excellence ceremony.

Congratulations to both of them. It is good to see this longstanding Burnley charity continuing to support young people alongside the organisations that contribute to the town's cultural and community life.
